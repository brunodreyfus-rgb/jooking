Travel Risks

Understand the incident types documented by Jooking and review the live country-level map.

Direct service discrimination

Refusal of accommodation, transport, restaurant service, airport assistance or another tourism service because a traveler is Jewish or Israeli.

Harassment and hostile atmosphere

Threats, intimidation, abuse, assault or a hostile environment affecting travelers in or around tourism locations.

Operational disruption

Protests, blockades, cancellations or other actions that specifically prevent Israeli or Jewish travelers from accessing a destination or service.

Evidence and confidence

Reports are reviewed before publication. Risk levels reflect the number and nature of approved reports and do not describe every person or business in a country.
